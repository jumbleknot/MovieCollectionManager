- [ADR-0001: Production secrets-management standard](adr-0001-prod-secrets-management.md) - The ratified decision record selecting Komodo Variables (not HashiCorp Vault) as the sanctioned production secrets mechanism for all core stacks, with Vault kept dormant and agent-layer-scoped only.

- [ADR-0003: Grounded Claims and protected-passage fingerprints are complements](adr-0003-claims-and-fingerprints.md) - The governance decision that Grounded Claims and the protected.yaml fingerprints are complements with disjoint scopes — Claims on derived pages only, fingerprints on authoritative pages only, never both. Its Claims half is superseded by ADR-0004; its fingerprint half stands.
- [ADR-0004: Wiki summaries are maintained by the coding session](adr-0004-session-maintained-wiki.md) - The decision (feature 098) that a derived page is refreshed by the session that changes its source, in the same PR, with a source fingerprint and the blocking V17 check in place of Claims and a scheduled generator.
